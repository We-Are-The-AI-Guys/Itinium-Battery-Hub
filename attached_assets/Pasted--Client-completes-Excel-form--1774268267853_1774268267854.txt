2. Process Improvement Version (Optimised Workflow)
1. Client Submission
•	Client completes smart Excel form or online submission form with validation rules.
•	Automatic email routing to Collections.
________________________________________
2. Automated Job Creation
•	System auto generates a job number upon receipt.
•	Data from the form auto populates internal logs, eliminating manual re entry.
•	Collections team only reviews exceptions flagged by the system.
________________________________________
3. Streamlined Scheduling
•	Collections team proposes available dates via template email or automated scheduler.
•	Client confirmation automatically updates the job record.
•	Driver and vehicle assignment selected from a drop down resource list.
________________________________________
4. Collection Execution
•	Driver receives job details via mobile app or automated job sheet.
•	Driver uploads collection confirmation (photo, signature, weights if applicable).
________________________________________
5. Receipt & Adjustments
•	Receipt automatically logged when driver submits confirmation.
•	Any discrepancies flagged for manual review.
________________________________________
6. Automated Client Documentation
•	System generates: 
o	Collection sheet
o	Accounts Excel file
•	Documents sent automatically to the client with job reference.
________________________________________
7. Digital Filing
•	All documents stored in a single digital folder under the job number.
•	No manual sorting; system enforces naming conventions.
________________________________________
8. Job Closure
•	Job auto closes once all required documents are present.
•	Exceptions flagged for manual intervention.
