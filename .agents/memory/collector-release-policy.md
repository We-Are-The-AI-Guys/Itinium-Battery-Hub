---
name: Itinium approval before collector visibility
description: CBL must only see and be notified of collection jobs after Itinium releases the reviewed collection.
---

Client submission is not permission to expose a job to CBL. Itinium must review collection details and the collection table, then explicitly use “Submit for collection.” Collector dashboards must show total collection weight and pallet count for vehicle/driver planning.

**Why:** The user reports premature collector visibility and notifications risk scheduling the wrong vehicle before Itinium has corrected review items and load details.

**How to apply:** Separate collection release from client submission, scheduling confirmation, and receipt confirmation. Gate collector access and notifications on release, not merely assignment to a collection company.
