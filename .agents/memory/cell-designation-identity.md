---
name: Cell designation identity
description: How to use catalogue equivalence without silently altering a battery's entered identity.
---

Keep a manually entered or deliberately selected designation as the recorded product identity, even when a manufacturer's equivalent or a TAB fallback is available. A shorthand prefix is a search query, not a uniquely identified cell.

**Why:** Different manufacturers can share comparable cell families while assigning distinct model names and Pb figures. Silent canonicalisation made typed designations and explicit choices turn into another model, obscuring what the collector actually reported.

**How to apply:** Offer matching models as explicit suggestions. Use a manufacturer-specific catalogue match, then TAB if appropriate, for weight/Pb calculations only. Flag unresolved identity for authorised review rather than converting the saved designation to a calculation fallback.
